Coach visual — three concepts

All three run on their own clock at the tempo below, no scrolling involved. They simulate a player who drifts: the timing error wanders, so you can watch each one respond. Obsidian palette, same tokens as the site.

Concept 1

The Pocket Dial

One bar around the ring, ticks for subdivisions. A hit lands inside the ring when it is early and outside when it is late, so the shape of the cloud is your timing. Closest to the rings-and-ticks language you liked.

Concept 3

Pendulum Wave

Pendulums of slightly different lengths: in phase, then fanning apart, then converging again. It is about time itself rather than about your playing — the best object, the least specific claim.

Concept 2

The Drift Ribbon

The strip moves itself, right to left. The centre line is perfect time; hits sit above it when rushing and below when dragging, and the ribbon traces the trend. The clearest teacher of the three — you can read "you are dragging" without a caption.

Rough prototypes, deliberately: enough to judge each as an object before any of it is built properly. Hit values are simulated, not real playing.
