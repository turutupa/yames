Privacy Policy

Short version: Yames does not collect anything. There is no account, no analytics, and nothing about your practice ever leaves your device.

Last updated September 11, 2026.

What Yames stores

Yames keeps its settings, presets, setlists, and drill history in a small file on your own device — the same place any app keeps its preferences. This is what lets Yames remember your last tempo, your saved exercises, and your practice history between sessions. It is never uploaded, backed up to a server, or shared with anyone, including the people who make Yames.

What Yames sends anywhere else

Nothing. Yames does not:

  • create an account or ask for one
  • run analytics, crash reporting, or advertising
  • send your practice data, presets, or setlists anywhere
  • require an internet connection to work

The desktop version can optionally check GitHub for app updates and, if you turn on the practice coach, download a model file once — both are actions you start, both only talk to GitHub, and neither sends anything about you. The mobile version does not do either: app updates come from the app store you installed it from, and mobile does not include the practice coach.

Permissions

The mobile app does not request the microphone, camera, contacts, location, or any other sensitive permission. It asks only for what the operating system requires to play sound and, on Android, to keep the click running while the screen is off during a practice session.

Children

Yames is a practice tool with no accounts, no user-generated content shared with others, and no ads. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — for example, if a future version adds an optional online feature — this page will say so before that version ships, with the date above updated.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about Yames: github.com/turutupa/yames/issues.
