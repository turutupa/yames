WHAT CHANGES UNDERNEATH Raw is synthesised like the others but shaped for rock: a click layer on the kick, a saturated snare body, a compressed envelope on both, hats short and bright, no room tail. Loud now changes the pattern, not only the level: ghosts go, hats open on the off-beats, a crash lands on section starts. And a new jam starts with drums alone.
