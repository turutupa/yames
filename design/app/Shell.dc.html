THE SHELL One rail, one transport, and nothing that jumps Today five surfaces compete to navigate: a tab strip, a preset drawer, seven unlabelled icons, a floating play button and a coach rail. The proposal collapses them into two fixed frames that never move, and lets everything else open inside them.

The rule that fixes the jump The tempo readout sits at a fixed distance from the rail, not in the middle of whatever space is left. Open the coach, open the presets, resize the window — the number you are staring at stays exactly where your eye already is. Today it slides sideways every time a panel opens.
