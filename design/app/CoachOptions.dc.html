A · Dock A column on the right, roughly where it is today but wider and built for a conversation rather than a feed.

B · Centre stage The coach takes the middle. The click keeps running as a live strip along the top, so nothing stops to talk.
Room for evidence: charts, tab, a real answer. The big beat dots shrink — wrong while you are mid-take.

COACH
C · Floor A band across the bottom, under the tempo. Full width, so a chart or a bar of tab fits end to end.
Tempo stays the hero; wide enough for notation later. Height is the scarce dimension on a laptop, and paths need it.
THE PROPOSAL A and B are one panel at two sizes, not two designs. It docks while the click runs and takes the centre the moment you stop — because that is exactly when you have a question and nothing to watch. One button, one keystroke, one footswitch action moves it either way.
