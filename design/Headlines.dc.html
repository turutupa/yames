For when the coach ships
05

The metronome that listens back.

The bet: still sells a metronome — the word a musician is searching for — while making the coach the reason this one is different. The bridge between today's positioning and tomorrow's.
The risk: it writes a cheque the beta has to cash. Hold it until the coach is polished, then it becomes the obvious headline.
Metronome or coach?
Keep selling a metronome for now. It is the word musicians actually search for, it is a promise the app already keeps completely, and the coach is still beta — leading with it would set an expectation the current build has to meet on the first session. The coach stays where it is: the section that makes people choose this metronome over the free one they already have.

When the coach is polished, the switch is a headline and a hero section, not a redesign — candidate 05 is written for exactly that day.
