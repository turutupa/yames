03 — practice coach Beta

A measurement of how you actually played.

It listens through the microphone, scores the session, and tells you the thing a metronome normally can't: whether you were dragging or rushing, by how many milliseconds, and where it fell apart. The model runs locally — the app makes no network calls at all.

04 — zen mode

And then, the opposite of a spec sheet.

Seven fullscreen visuals that move on the beat, in any of the ten themes. No numbers, no controls, nothing to read. Put the laptop across the room and play.

05 — control surface

Hands stay on the instrument.

Map any MIDI CC, note or program change to play, stop, tempo or subdivision. Assign a footswitch and run the whole session without looking up. Every action has a rebindable hotkey.

06 — finish

Ten themes, widget included.

Dark and light, serif and monospace. The always-on-top floating widget follows the theme and sits over your DAW, your tabs, or your sheet music.
