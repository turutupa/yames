## Sentences (English) and what each quotes

- beatPositionBias: "Beat {beat} of the bar came {ms} ms early|late on average, out of line with the
  other beats." (`beatPosition`, that beat's mean deviation against the click).
- subdivisionWeak: "No note landed on the {position} in {missed} of {total} beats." (`onsets - hits`
  of `onsets` at `subdivisionPosition`).
- tempoCeiling: "Your timing held at {holds} BPM and slipped at {breaks} BPM." (`bpmBand`, 10 BPM bands).
- drift: "By the last third of the run you were landing {ms} ms earlier|later than in the first
  third." (last third's mean deviation minus the first third's).

## Decisions

- `findings` is on the event's payload type, **not** on `SessionReport` (the brief said
  `SessionReport.findings?`): the mini-report's report is pushed into `segmentReportsRef` and saved,
  so a field there would be stored. The event is the only carrier.
- The listener lives in `ToolCoach.tsx`, so `useSession.ts` / `useSegmentCoach.ts` are untouched.
- Drift is quoted in ms, not BPM: the analyzer measures the shift of the mean deviation, not tempo.
- "Nothing measured" includes a segment under 10 s (segment's first to last onset) and |ms| < 3.
