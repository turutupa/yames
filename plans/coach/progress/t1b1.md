## Decisions

- **The way back** of a `go` tool is good on the screen the receipt left the player on (`goTarget`: the
  receipt's screen, Paths for a Path or Step, Settings); a view change (`useAskHost`'s effect on `view`)
  takes it away. A confirmed change's Undo: 10 s (`UNDO_MS`), and only on the screen it was issued on (the
  view change takes it away; the runner refuses it anywhere else, and a refused Undo says "The way back is
  no longer there"). A `notNow` (the click still playing) keeps it and says why. Undone: "Undone." and
  the undo's own receipt.
- **A proposal is replaced by what came of the press** (the receipt, or the runner's refusal for a stale
  one: "That has changed since, so nothing was done"); "Not now" leaves it, muted, with "Not now: nothing
  was changed."
- **A preset's receipt** says "Loading {preset}…" until the app's re-read answers, then "Loaded" or
  "{preset} is gone, so nothing was loaded". A late answer during a Take is held (`settleAskTurn`).
- **No chip for a tool that answers "not yet"** (`loop_bars`, `make_exercise`, `explain_screen`,
  `set_goal`). "Which one?" and "that one is yours" are reached by t1b2's typed commands; until then by
  `runAskCall` / `askYours` (the layout scenes call them).
- **Show me marks the control**: `[data-coach-point]` (an accent outline) on the Settings section's element
  for 2.4 s, set 0.7 s after the press (Settings' own scroll nudges end at 0.52 s).
- Renamed / added keys beyond t1a1/t1a2's proposals: see `lineKey` above, plus `receipt.loadingPreset`,
  `receipt.presetGone`, `chip.*`, `verb.*`, `stops.*`, `yours.*`, `source.*`, `ui.*`, `screen.*`,
  `section.*`, `setting.*`, `value.*`, `undo.generic`.

## Review fixes (leg t1b1b; review in `/tmp/coach-orch/t1b1-review.md`)

- **S1** An open proposal does not outlive its screen: the proposal body records the view it was made on
  (`host.view()`) and the Session step (`ctx.run.stepIndex`). `noteAskToolView(view, step?)` dismisses
  through the runner (`dismissProposal`) every open proposal whose view differs (or is unknown), or whose
  step differs when the step is given; state `expired`, the card says `coachTools.why.refused.proposal`
  ("That has changed since, so nothing was done. Ask again.") and has no button. `useAskHost` now calls it
  on `[view, step]` (step = the run's `stepIndex` in a Session, else null; it changes per step, never per
  beat). Tests: `askTools.test.tsx` "does not outlive its screen: start_today ..." and "a view note on its
  own screen leaves it open; in a Session, the step moving on retires it".
- **N1** Chips memo keyed on the store's `turns` identity (`AskPanel.tsx`). Test: `AskPanel.test.tsx`
  "the chips are chosen again when a turn is written in place ...".
- **N2** `loadPresetAfterReRead` (`src/containers/coach/coachPresetLoad.ts`) awaits `handleLoadPreset`
  (which now resolves true when every step applied) before `applied`; a failed step or a throw is
  `applied("failed")` -> new key `coachTools.receipt.presetFailed` (15 locales). `applied` is heard once.
  Tests: `coachPresetLoad.test.ts`; `askTools.test.tsx` "a preset that did not take ...".
- **N3** `settleAskTurn` takes a change function as well as a turn; held changes apply to the turn as it is
  on release. `followLater` holds only the settled line. Test: `askTools.test.tsx` "a late answer held
  through a Take ...".
- **Double press**: `ask-tools.spec.ts` "a fast double-click has one effect ...". The proposal's button
  and a chip in the empty state passed as they were; a chip in the row above the text box ran twice
  (3 tool turns for 2). Fix: `onePress` (`askTools.ts`) on every tool button in Ask ignores a click with
  `detail > 1`, so the second click neither repeats a call nor presses a button drawn under the pointer
  by the first (key presses have detail 0). Unit test: `askTools.test.tsx` "the second click of a
  double-click is not a press ...".
- Gates (logs `/tmp/coach-t1b1/b-*.log`): tsc clean; vitest coach files 538/538, then the whole suite
  21799 passed (only the known `TabStage.render.test.tsx` load failure); build green; layout
  `ask-tools`, `ask`, `smooth` 51 passed. The full layout suite is owed (after 8 PM).
