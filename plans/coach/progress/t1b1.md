## Decisions

- **The way back** of a `go` tool is good on the screen the receipt left the player on (`goTarget`: the
  receipt's screen, Paths for a Path or Step, Settings); a view change (`useAskHost`'s effect on `view`)
  takes it away. A confirmed change's Undo: 10 s (`UNDO_MS`), and only on the screen it was issued on (the
  view change takes it away; the runner refuses it anywhere else, and a refused Undo says "The way back is
  no longer there"). A `notNow` (the click still playing) keeps it and says why. Undone: "Undone." and
  the undo's own receipt.
- **A proposal is replaced by what came of the press** (the receipt, or the runner's refusal for a stale
  one: "That has changed since, so nothing was done"); "Not now" leaves it, muted, with "Not now: nothing
  was changed."
- **A preset's receipt** says "Loading {preset}…" until the app's re-read answers, then "Loaded" or
  "{preset} is gone, so nothing was loaded". A late answer during a Take is held (`settleAskTurn`).
- **No chip for a tool that answers "not yet"** (`loop_bars`, `make_exercise`, `explain_screen`,
  `set_goal`). "Which one?" and "that one is yours" are reached by t1b2's typed commands; until then by
  `runAskCall` / `askYours` (the layout scenes call them).
- **Show me marks the control**: `[data-coach-point]` (an accent outline) on the Settings section's element
  for 2.4 s, set 0.7 s after the press (Settings' own scroll nudges end at 0.52 s).
- Renamed / added keys beyond t1a1/t1a2's proposals: see `lineKey` above, plus `receipt.loadingPreset`,
  `receipt.presetGone`, `chip.*`, `verb.*`, `stops.*`, `yours.*`, `source.*`, `ui.*`, `screen.*`,
  `section.*`, `setting.*`, `value.*`, `undo.generic`.

## Chips per screen

| Screen | Chips (at most 4, then each tool's `when`) |
|---|---|
| Today | Start today's Session (with a plan), Open {Path}, Step {n} (the plan's first Exercise), Go to Paths, Open Settings · Practice |
| Paths | Play {first Exercise} (on a Step page) or Open {Path}, Step {n}; Start today's Session; Go to Today |
| Progress | Start today's Session, Go to Paths, Go to Today |
| Tools | Go to Metronome, Drill, Jam, Songs |
| Metronome, Drill | Slower, Faster, Two clicks a beat / One click a beat, Go to Today |
| Jam | Slower, Faster, Go to Today |
| Setlist | Go to Today, Open Settings · Practice |
| Songs | Show bars 1 to 4 (a song open), Go to Today |
| Session (ready, result) | Slower / Faster (where they mean anything), Open Settings · Practice |
| A Take | none (Ask is not mounted) |

## Open points

- Paths' place moving by hand inside Paths does not take a way back away (only a view change does).
- The chips are chosen again when the screen key or the thread changes, not when Paths moves to a Step
  page while Ask stays open on Paths.
- Words worth a native read: `yours.*`, `verb.*` (de "Los", fr "Y aller"), `value.view.runway`
  (left "Runway" in de), the Settings section names (translators did not all check Settings' titles).
