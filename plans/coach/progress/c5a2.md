## English

- "Bar {{bar}} of {{song}} is due today." / "Bars {{from}} to {{to}} of {{song}} are due today." Measured: the `score_due` row (song id, played bars, `dueDay` <= today local) and the library's name for the song.
- The Songs note adds no sentence: its headline is the existing verdict; fact line reuses "no extras" / "N extra notes" / "N% right notes (of M I could pitch)".

## Decisions

- No bar strip in the Songs note: the review's bars are already the `tabExcerpt` block in the body (the scrolling half), and a song pass can be 100+ bars; the pinned head stays sentence + source + facts + fix.
- The due list is read with `listDue()` directly (as the brief says), not `listSongDue()`, so Today never runs the settings-to-store move (a write).
- A passage across a repeat is said as the lowest to the highest printed bar it covers (played 3..6 over a repeat of 1..4 is "bars 2 to 5").
