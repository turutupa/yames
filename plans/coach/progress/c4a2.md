## Every store write, and the press behind it

- `saveSuppressed(line.id, now)`: the Leave out button of that line.
- `saveUnsuppressed(line.id)`: the Undo beside "Left out for 30 days" (offered 10 s).
- `saveCardDismissed("goal", now)`: Not now on the goal question.
- `saveGoal` via `set_goal`: Save in the goal form (`runAskCall` then `pressProposal` in the same press; the receipt and Undo are the runner's, shown under the goal and in Ask's thread).
- `saveSuggestions(value)`: a press on On / Only when I ask.
Nothing is written at the tab's open, at load, or by the goal question being shown (tested).

## Decisions

- Built on demand: the Takes are read once when the tab opens (`useState(sourceAtOpen)`); the book recomputes only when a coach key changes (a press). The panel is not mounted during a Take.
- In a Session: only Takes stored before the Session began, a line saying so, and never the goal question (6.6). Edit / Add a goal stay (a press).
- The goal question is the Notebook's only (c4b2 adds Today's card). It shows when `askGoal` (goal read and none, first Take 7+ days old, `cardMayShow`). After Not now: 7 days; after two, not until asked (store rules). "Add a goal" is always there when the goal is read: a press, not an offer.
- Not now has no Undo (there is no un-dismiss writer; `saveCardAsked` clears all dismissals, which is not the same). Its note names Settings → Coach → Suggestions. Leave out's note does not name Settings: Settings has no list of lines left out, so naming it would point at nothing (deviates from the brief's wording; the owner may want such a list in c6).
- States: loading = "One moment: reading the coach's notes." (both sections); suppressed unreadable = says so, no line; goal unreadable = says so, no offer, no Add; a press that returns `notRead` or settles `notSaved` says so.
- Suggestions row: while the key loads neither button shows chosen; unreadable says so and a press saves the whole value (store allows it).
