## The four keys

| Key | Shape | Default (missing, `null`, malformed) | Written by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `coach.suggestions` | `"on"` \| `"asked"` | `"on"` | `saveSuggestions`: `set_setting coachSuggestions` (confirm), Settings' row (c4a2) |
| `coach.cards` | `{ [kind]: { lastShown: ms\|null, dismissed: int, lastDismissed: ms\|null } }`; kinds `milestone`, `weekly`, `plateau`, `goal` (an unknown kind read from the store is kept) | `{}` | `saveCardShown` (when shown), `saveCardDismissed` (Hide / Not now), `saveCardAsked` (the player asked for it: dismissals cleared) |
| `coach.goal` | `{ text (trimmed, 1–200 chars), at: ms\|null }` | `null` (no goal) | `saveGoal`: `set_goal` (confirm, and its undo) |
| `coach.suppressed` | `{ [lineId]: ms left out }` | `{}` | `saveSuppressed` (Leave out; ids past their 30 days are dropped at that write), `saveUnsuppressed` (its Undo) |

Why its own module and not beside `CoachSettingsSection.tsx`: that section writes its keys inline and reads none
of them (they are read in MainWindow and the loader). These four are read by the tool host, the Notebook and the
card budget, so one module owns their shape. Every `storeLoad` / `storeSave` names its key as a literal, so the
parity scan sees them.

Rules: nothing written at load, by a default or by a migration; a stored `null` reads as "no choice"; a key that
fails to read is its default and the rest still load. A press made before the load finished is kept over what the
load reads. The host answers `notWiredYet` for the goal and Suggestions until the load has finished, so no proposal
is made over a goal it has not read.

6.6 as functions: `cardMayShow(stored, kind, now)`: false when `suggestions === "asked"`, after two dismissals
(until `withCardAsked`), or within seven days of the last dismissal; being shown alone holds nothing back. "One card
per app open" is the caller's (c4b2). 6.7: `isSuppressed(suppressed, id, now)`: thirty days.

## The tendency kinds

Every line: at least five Takes; Takes marked not fair left out of everything; Takes on Path `mine` (t2b) never
read; only the newest 500 rows; ids in `coach.suppressed` left out for thirty days. Each carries `id`, `kind`,
`key` + `values`, `evidence` (`notebook.evidence.oneExercise` / `.exercises`, `count`, `exercises`; `from` / `to`
are for c4a2 to fill with the window's dates), `exercises`, `takeIds`, `from`, `to`.

| Kind | Id | Reads | When it is a line |
|---|---|---|---|
| tempo against evenness | `tempoEvenness:<fast>:<slow>` | graded, heard Takes' `measures.evenness` | Two tempos with ≥ 2 Takes each on the same Exercise; evenness ≥ 5 points lower at the faster on **every** Exercise that has both; ≥ 5 Takes. The pair with the most Takes. |
| lean by bar position | `lean:<first\|last>:<late\|early>` | `lean` (≥ 2 pattern bars) | Of the newest 10 Takes with a value there (≥ 5), ≥ 60% past `biasThresholdMs(bpm)` one way. |
| lean by place | `place:<afterHeld\|openString\|shift>:<dir>` | `lean` + the Exercise's notes (variant applied) through `placeTriggers` | The mean lean of the bars that have it, in Takes that also have bars without it; the same 10 / 5 / 60%. Needs `paths`. |
| tempo played (ungraded) | `tempoPlayed:<path>/<exercise>` | ungraded Takes' `bpm` | ≥ 5 on one Exercise and the oldest and newest tempo differ. |
| minutes per Session | `minutes:sessions` | every Take's bars × tempo (meter from the Exercise, else 4/4, as Progress counts) | ≥ 4 of the newest 8 Sessions, newer half against older half differ by ≥ 2 whole minutes; ≥ 5 Takes. |

An ungraded or unheard Take carries no measure, so an ungraded player gets the last two kinds only (tested).

### Left out, and why

- **"Your eighths drop"** (the mock's subdivision word): a Take stores no subdivision; the line names the tempos only.
- **Notes heard, extras, right notes against tempo**: the same shape as evenness would work (`measures.heard`,
  `noteIdentity`), but the scorer's own pass bar differs per Exercise; left for a later leg rather than three more
  lines with thinner evidence.
- **String change, last bars as a place**: `stringChange` is in `placeTriggers` but not asked for; "last bars" is the
  position line already.
- **Upstroke, bends, vibrato, legato**: reported per Take, never graded (owner decision 3), and the per-Take counts
  are too sparse to compare.
- **Anything by time of day, day of week, or after a break**: needs no new fact but is not a comparison the design
  names; left as an idea.
- **"Takes on them" across Sessions vs within a Session (first Take vs later)**: possible from `session`, not built.

### One fixture history and its lines (`tendencies.test.ts`)

Two Exercises, each played twice at 100 and twice at 90 over six days (evenness 0.74 / 0.72 / 0.86 / 0.84 on one,
0.76 / 0.74 / 0.85 / 0.87 on the other) →

- `tempoEvenness:100:90`: "At 100 BPM your Takes were 74% even; at 90 BPM, 86%." / "From 8 Takes on 2 Exercises ·
  {from} to {to}".

Six Takes of the held-note fixture (lean `[0, -1, 30, -5]` ms at 100 BPM) →

- `place:afterHeld:late`: "In The held-note tune, bars where a note follows a held one sat late against the rest:
  6 of your last 6 Takes on it." / "From 6 Takes · {from} to {to}".

## Strings: to translate (c4a2)

All in `src/locales/*/notebook.json`, English in fourteen files: `notebook.noticed.tempoEvenness`,
`notebook.noticed.lean.{first,last}.{late,early}` (`_one` / `_other`), `notebook.noticed.place.{afterHeld,
openString,shift}.{late,early,lateOne,earlyOne}` (`_one` / `_other`), `notebook.noticed.tempoPlayed.{named,unnamed}`,
`notebook.noticed.minutes.perSession`, `notebook.evidence.{oneExercise,exercises}` (`_one` / `_other`). Plural on
`count`. Never "the bar" for the pass mark (linted). c4a2 adds its own keys to the same namespace.

## For c4a2 and c4b2

- Build on demand: `notebookFrom(progress.takes, coachStored(), Date.now(), session.paths)` when the tab opens.
  `coachStoredLoaded()` false: wait on `loadCoachStored()` (the host starts it on mount).
- React: `useSyncExternalStore(subscribeCoachStored, coachStored)`.
- `askGoal` = no goal, the oldest Take in the window ≥ 7 days old, and `cardMayShow(stored, "goal", now)`. Count the
  offer with `saveCardShown("goal", now)`, Not now with `saveCardDismissed("goal", now)`.
- Leave out: `saveSuppressed(line.id, now)`; Undo: `saveUnsuppressed(line.id)`.
- `values.exercise` is the pack's title (as `todayLine` passes it); the place line falls back to the plain key when
  the Exercise is not in the Paths given.
- `set_setting coachSuggestions` speaks on / off (off = "Only when I ask"); its undo writes the old value back
  explicitly, so after an undo from a never-chosen state the store holds `"on"`, which reads the same as no choice.
