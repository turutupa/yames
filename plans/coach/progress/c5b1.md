# c5b1: "Hear bar N", the engine half (progress)

Branch `coach-c5b1`. Option **A**, settled by the orchestrator: one "last heard Take" slot in RAM,
replaced by the next Take's audio, emptied when the input stops (`stop_evaluation`) or on
`forget_heard_take`; never on disk, never in an event; the recording session untouched; the tap is
armed on exactly the Takes it was armed on before (not option B). No UI (c5b2).

## What landed

## Decisions

- **Bars from the engine's own bar lines**, not `firstBeatMs + n * 60/bpm`: `BeatMap` logs where
  every bar opened, so a tempo step or a meter change lands where it happened. Bar k (1-based) is
  the k-th bar from beat 0, which is the first downbeat after the count-in. Only bars whose every
  quarter was logged and which lie wholly inside the audio are held, from bar 1 with no hole; the
  count-in is not a bar of its own (it is simply audio before bar 1).
- **Margins** (judgement call 2): 150 ms before the downbeat, 300 ms after the bar's end (an early
  attack; the last note's ring and the input's round trip, which puts attacks after their beats),
  clipped to the audio. A 5 ms linear fade at each cut edge (a declick, not a gain). Level: the raw
  input's. Not heard by a person yet; if it is too quiet, c5b2 reports it.
- **Memory**: the tap's buffer is sized for 120 s; `HeardTake::new` shrinks it to the Take's length
  on the notes thread (one copy, the rest freed there). 40 s at 48 kHz = 7.68 MB (test); 40 s at
  44.1 kHz = 7.056 MB (measured in the probe). For a moment two are alive while one replaces the
  other.
- **Races**: a Start cuts under the app state's lock, before `is_playing` rises; `hear_take` checks
  the transport and hands the bar over under the same lock, so a hear-back can never be started
  behind a Start. A pass running when the input stops cannot keep its audio after (an epoch moved by
  `forget`). An earlier Take's pass finishing late never replaces a later Take.
- **Refusals**: `hear_take` refuses with the transport running (a hear-back would silence a Take),
  while a jam take records, for a serial not held, and for a bar not held.
- A Take with no capture (input off, MIDI, a gap, no tap) leaves the previous Take's audio in the
  slot under its own serial: the frontend asks by serial, so it gets "no" for the new Take.
- The rhythm-only Take kept by the recording session (`start_capture_only`) has no `notes_serial`
  and is not kept for hearing back.

## Things the next leg must know (c5b2)

- `take-playback-ended` fires for a hear-back too, with the generation `hearTake` resolved to. A
  finished hear-back still holds the engine's take slot (the take branch runs before the click and
  outputs silence) until `stopHearTake(generation)` or a Start; call `stopHearTake` on its ending.
  While it holds the slot a jam take cannot start recording (`is_playing_back`).
- A device change goes through `stop_evaluation`, so it forgets the heard Take.
- `heardTake(serial)` is answered only once the pitch pass is done and the audio is moved (after
  the late grade); ask after `getTakeGradeWithNotes`.

## The probe

`--hear-take`: a fake 40 s Take in the slot; then the click 3 s, stop, two bars handed over as
`hear_take` does it, played out (and left finished in the slot) or cut by a Start half-way,
alternately, and a Start that cuts first. **Missed beats are counted over the click's spans only**:
a stopped or hear-back buffer returns before the probe's stamp and the engine's sample counter
starts again from 0 at each Start, so the run is cut into spans where the counter goes back, jitter
pairs are taken inside a span only, and missed beats are summed span by span. Allocations, frees
and dropped notifications are counted over everything, hear-back buffers included.

| run | load (1/5/15) at start | p99 ms | max ms | missed | allocs / frees | dropped | result |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--no-llm --hear-take --volume 0.01` (60 s, 26.6 s of click in 10 spans; 5 played out, 4 cut) | 43.3 / 45.9 / 51.9 (end 18.5) | 0.78 | 2.82 | 0 | 0 / 0 | 0 | PASS |

That run's device was at 44.1 kHz and the fake Take too, so it did not resample; the probe now
picks a rate that differs from the output's. The quiet-machine runs are the orchestrator's (see
the report for the commands).
