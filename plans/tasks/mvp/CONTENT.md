# Paths for the first testers (decided 2026-11-16)

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `plans/tasks/mvp/PLAN.md`, `plans/PATHS_PIPELINE.md` (the format, the pipeline, the
checks), then this. The panel's inputs are in `review-paths/` (CONTEXT.md, CURRENT_CONTENT.md).

## The owner's goals

Learning Paths must visibly help two kinds of player from the first test:

- **Mechanics:** a shredder: alternate picking, economy picking, legato, sweeps, everything shred.
- **Theory learnt through exercises:** a blues player, a classic rock player.

The test needs about three base Paths, even if incomplete. "Play now" feeds exercises from all
subscribed Paths, so the player advances on all of them daily with one click.

The owner's further guidance:
- Content may follow what established courses teach; most courses are similar, and that's fine.
- Repetitive drills are fine, especially for shred: muscle memory is the point.

## What the panel agreed (an expert teacher, an intermediate guitarist, a product reviewer)

1. **Base Paths: Shred, Blues, Classic Rock.**
   - **Shred:** one Path of 15 steps that rotates the techniques (the teacher's outline, below). The orchestrator chose this over a goal grouping separate Paths, for the owner's single "shredder path".
   - **Alternate Picking and Sweep Picking** stay as optional deep-dive Paths.
2. **Theory is taught through written licks and phrases, graded on timing, notes heard and right notes.**
   - Landing, scale-membership and free-improvising exercises become a small "your turn" minority.
   - These are self-checked and labelled "not checked yet" until those graders exist.
   - Nothing may claim grading it doesn't have.
   - Every written lick gets a timing bar; a swung lick gets a swing-tolerant spread.
3. **Honest labels:**
   - Legato: missed hammer-ons and pull-offs cost nothing. Tie legato to picked anchors, pair each legato bar with a picked bar of the same notes, and add a self-check: "did the third note sound as loud as the first?"
   - Sweeps: mostly timing only, since right notes read "not checked". Say "timing only" in the label.
   - Pick direction is never heard: it is a checklist item.
4. **Tempos:**
   - Every Exercise shows its subdivision.
   - Starting and pass tempos must be comparable across neighbouring Exercises.
   - Shred needs sextuplets and 16th triplets, and pass tempos high enough for intermediate shred: one-string 16ths to about 110–120.
5. **Progression:**
   - A step is done when about 3 of its 4 graded Exercises are passed; the rest go to reviews.
   - Placement or test-out: one Take at the pass tempo passes an Exercise, and the first Session offers Find my edge on early Steps, so intermediate players skip what they know.
6. **Scope:**
   - At least 20–25 graded Exercises per base Path, so 2–4 weeks of daily use never runs dry.
   - Unbuilt later Steps are listed as "coming in the next update", never locked if built.
   - If a Path runs out, its minutes go to reviews and Find my edge.

## The Shred Path (the teacher's outline)

Notation:
- Strings 1 (high e) to 6 (low E); BPM is quarter notes; A minor unless noted.
- Grading: T timing, H notes heard, N right notes (notes ≥ 75 ms apart only), S self-check.

| # | Step | Exercises (pattern · subdivision · start→pass BPM · bars) | Grading |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | One-string 16ths (keep Alternate Picking 1–2) | (a) Open A, 16ths, 60→110, 8 bars. (b) e: 12-13-15-13 looped, 16ths, 60→120. (c) 1-2-3-4 chromatic on G from fret 5, 60→110 | T H N |
| 2 | Accents and groupings (keep AP3) | (a) e 8-7-5-7-8-7 sextuplets, accent beat 1, 50→90. (b) the same six as 16ths so the accent walks (3 against 4), 55→90. (c) 16th triplets on open A, accent every third note, 50→80 | T H N + accent placement |
| 3 | Petrucci chromatic and spider | (a) 1-2-3-4 per string from 6 to 1 at fret 5, up a fret each pass, 16ths, 55→100. (b) spider on 6+5, fingers 1-3 then 2-4, crawling, 50→90. (c) the 1-3-2-4 permutation, 50→90 | T H N |
| 4 | Crossing two strings (AP4 merged to 3) | outside pair, inside pair, the all-outside lick, 50→100 | T H N; direction S |
| 5 | Gilbert pentatonic crossings | (a) six-note loop B5-B8-e5-e8-e5-B8, sextuplets, 50→90. (b) the same loop down the box on 2-3, 3-4, 4-5. (c) the four-note B5-B8-e5-e8, 16ths, 60→110 | T H N |
| 6 | Three notes per string (AP6) | the six-note lick both ways; three strings up and down; 2½ octaves at 5th position, 16ths, 55→100 | T H N |
| 7 | 3nps sequences | (a) groups of 3 as triplets, 60→110. (b) groups of 4 as 16ths ("A minor in fours"), 50→90. (c) groups of 6, sextuplets, 45→80. (d) descending 4s, 50→85 | T H N |
| 8 | Speed bursts (AP7) | one beat on, one off, 80→130; two on, two off; expanding bursts | T H N |
| 9 | String skipping | (a) octaves (AP8). (b) pentatonic skip E5-8 / D5-7 / A5-7 / G5-7 / D7-5 / B5-8, 16ths, 50→90. (c) Am triad over a skipped string D7-10, B10-13, e12 and back, 50→85 | T H N |
| 10 | Economy picking | (a) 3nps ascending D-U-D then the crossing downstroke, 16ths 60→110. (b) a five-note group (e5-8, B5-6-8), quintuplets 45→80. (c) picked Am-F-C-G arpeggios (AP8), 50→100 | T H N; direction S |
| 11 | Legato I: trills and pulls | (a) trill G5-7, 16ths, picked accent every beat, 60→100, 16 bars. (b) e5h7h8p7p5 loop, picking only the first note of each beat, 60→100. (c) pentatonic pull-offs e8p5, B8p5, G7p5…, sextuplets 50→85 | T on picked beats; H soft; N where spaced; volume S |
| 12 | Legato II: 3nps runs | (a) hammer-only ascent with hammer-on-from-nowhere, 3nps 55→90. (b) pull-off descent. (c) a picked bar then a legato bar, same notes | T H N on the picked bar; S "same volume?" |
| 13 | Sweep basics (Sweep 1–3) | two-string rolls; Am on three strings both ways; the turnaround at fret 8; triplets 50→90 | T H; extras catch ringing; muting S; "timing only" |
| 14 | Five-string sweeps and links (Sweep 5 and 8) | Am classic shape from fret 12; the link; Am-F-C-G one pass per chord | as 13 |
| 15 | Tapping, then the etude (AP9–10) | (a) e 12t-5-8 triplets, 60→110. (b) tap-pull-hammer over Am-F-G, the tap moving per chord. (c) the stamina minute. (d) the eight-bar run over the band, 60→100 | T on taps; the run T H N |

Pass bars should vary with difficulty:
- Easy steps demand tighter evenness.
- Finales demand longer Takes (16 bars).

## Blues (rewrite the draft; about 75% gradable)

1. **Box 1:** keep up/down, the three A's and the first phrase. Replace "Free in the box" with a written two-bar lick.
2. **Chord tones as written arpeggios:** A7, D7 and E7 in box 1, each followed by a written lick ending on the 3rd. Landing exercises stay S.
3. **Call and response:** keep Echo; add four written answers to one call; two bars on, two off as written phrases. Free trade stays practice-only.
4. **Blues scale:** keep its three written exercises.
5. **The 12-bar:** keep the roots per bar, the boogie line and the turnaround; add a written 12-bar chorus.
6. **Bends:** "bends on the beat" graded T H; a bend-and-release lick whose release note is graded N; the bend's pitch is S.
7. **Question and answer:** keep both written exercises.
8. **Box 2:** keep.
9. **Guide tones:** keep. It's the best step, fully gradable.
10. **Slow blues:** the written chorus graded T H N with a loose timing bar.

## Classic Rock (new; about 80% gradable)

1. **Riffs in 8ths:**
   - Smoke-style 4ths on D/G.
   - Power-chord root lines (Iron Man/Paranoid-type) as single notes.
   - 80→130, T H N.
2. **Pentatonic with the box 1 extension:**
   - The Page lick B8-5, G7-5, 16ths 60→110.
   - The Clapton string 1/2 repeat.
   - Slash-style descending 6s.
3. **Major pentatonic:**
   - A major pentatonic at fret 2, and box 1 at 14.
   - An Allman-style written lick.
   - Minor vs major written phrases over A5.
4. **Repeating licks (Page, Angus):**
   - Triplet pull-off loops e8p5, B8p5 (graded like legato).
   - An Angus string-2/1 open-string lick.
   - 80→140.
5. **Double stops and Chuck Berry:** written phrases, T H, with N on the separated notes.
6. **Hendrix-style chord embellishment:** hammered 3rds over E, written; T H.
7. **Chord tones over I-bVII-IV (A-G-D):** written arpeggios, then a lick ending on each chord's root.
8. **Gilmour phrasing:** a slow written melody of long notes, T H N, with an S checklist for bend and vibrato.
9. **Call and response over a rock groove:** written answers.
10. **The solo etude:** a 16-bar written solo with the band, then a free solo (S).

No licensed material: write original licks in the style, with nothing a reviewer would recognise as a specific song's notes.

## Play now across all subscribed Paths (the product reviewer's algorithm, adopted)

```
plan(minutes, P, progress, now):
  slots = SHAPE[minutes]     # 5: [5]; 10: warm 2, 4, 4; 15: warm 2, 5, 4, 4; 20: warm 2, 6, 4, 4, fun/review 4
  W = {p: worklist(p) for p in P if worklist(p) nonempty}
  focus = the Path that has waited longest to be focus (ties: the player's order); a Path unplayed for 4+ days jumps the queue
  warm-up from focus (or recap after a break)
  blocks = [focus block (its head Exercise, mid-climb if any)]
  others = P minus focus, by lastPlayedAt ascending
  due = passed Exercises whose spaced interval (1, 3, 7, 14, 30 days) has come
  for each remaining slot:
    20-minute last slot: a fun finisher with the band (a riff, lick or etude at a tempo already passed), else a review, else Find my edge
    else the next other Path's head Exercise; else a due review; else the focus Path's next Exercise
  skipped Paths get a reason ("Blues: played yesterday, back tomorrow")
```

Rules:
- One Exercise per Path per Session, at most 4 graded Exercises, blocks of at least 3 minutes.
- An Exercise with 3 Sessions in a row without a new clean rung is parked behind the next open one, and comes back in 2 days ("coming back fresh").
- A review is 2 Takes at the stored clean tempo: a pass moves it to the next interval; a fail marks it "slipped" and puts it back one rung below.
- At most 4 subscribed Paths in the test.
- Self-checked "your turn" items get an occasional 2-minute block.
- New reasons (all translated): `focusTurn`, `keepWarm`, `reviewDue`, `parked`, `skippedToday`, `finisher`, `placement`.

## Measuring whether it helps (all local; the tester sends it)

- **A benchmark Take** on days 1, 7, 14 and 21: the same Exercise at the same tempo, tagged so it can be compared.
- **Right notes on the first Take** of each new written phrase, early steps against later ones.
- **A one-tap "was this grade fair?"** on the result screen.
- **An export:** Takes, ceilings, benchmark tags, fairness answers and the planner's reasons, as a file the tester can attach to the existing "Send feedback".
- A weekly 1–5 confidence question per Path is optional.
