Jam kits — A/B

The same two bars of rock, played by each kit in turn, so they can be compared by ear rather than by table. Every file is peak 0.900; the balance between voices comes from each kit's own trim_db and the dynamics from the engine's level gains, so what you hear is the difference between the kits and nothing else.

This page needs a local server. Opened straight off disk, the browser refuses to read the WAVs. From the repository root run python -m http.server 8123 and then open http://localhost:8123/scripts/sounds/ab.html.

Click a kit while it is looping and it swaps at the top of the next bar — that is the comparison that matters. A recorded kit is about 18 MB and is fetched the first time you ask for it.

Blind test picks a kit at random and hides which. Judge it, then reveal.

The band

The same two bars, with a bass line and a Rhodes comp over them — Am to G. A recorded voice is a folder of sampled notes and the ones between them are built here the way the engine builds them, by playing the nearest sample back at 2n/12; a note is cut at the next one and faded over the bank's own release_ms, because a bass note that ran its full two seconds would be four notes deep by the end of the bar. Levels are the engine's: each voice's trim_db, then BASS_VOICE_TRIM and the keys' 0.07, which is what holds a four-note chord under a drummer.

The percussionist

One set, played under whichever kit is above — a percussionist is not a drum kit, so switching kits does not switch this. A shaker on every sixteenth, a conga tumbao and a tambourine on 2 and 4: the shaker is the voice most likely to turn into a hiss, the tumbao is where a machine-gun round robin shows up, and the tambourine has to survive the snare it lands on. The shaker's two round robins are the up-stroke and the down-stroke, so the sixteenths really do alternate. Levels and trim_db are the engine's, and each voice is placed by the pan in its own manifest, which is a number to argue with rather than something baked into the files.

One voice at a time

Plays the accent layer, then round robin 2, then the ghost layer, so the layers can be heard against each other. Faded voices are ones this kit does not have; the engine falls back for those and so does this page.

The groove

voicebar 1bar 2trim dB

Positions are 16ths; 1 e & a. A is a peak, x an accent, + a plain hit and o a ghost — the engine's five levels. Hats alternate strong and weak across the eighths, there are ghost notes under the backbeat, and the fill ramps into the crash on the one.
