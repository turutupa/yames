Jam kits — A/B

The same two bars of rock, played by each kit in turn, so the five can be compared by ear rather than by table. Every voice is peak 0.900 in the file; the mix below applies one set of per-voice levels to all kits, the way the engine does, so what you hear is the difference between the files and nothing else.

This page needs a local server. Opened straight off disk, the browser refuses to read the WAVs. From the repository root run python -m http.server 8123 and then open http://localhost:8123/scripts/sounds/ab.html.

Click a kit while it is looping and it swaps at the top of the next bar — that is the comparison that matters.

Blind test picks a kit at random and hides which. Judge it, then reveal.

One voice at a time

For naming what to change. The knobs behind each voice are listed in generate_sounds.py under RAW_SPEC — "more wire", "more click", "less ring" each point at one number.

The groove

voicebar 1bar 2level

Straight 8ths, backbeat on 2 and 4, the kick pushing the & of 3, two ghost notes so the soft snare is audible, an open hat lifting the end of each bar, and a crash on the one. Positions are 16ths; 1 e & a.
