Click presets — three strokes

Every preset ships a downbeat, a middle and a plain beat. This plays them on their own, and plays the bar they were built for, with the gains the engine uses and nothing else — no reverb, no humanising, no subdivision ticks.

This page needs a local server. Opened straight off disk, the browser refuses to read the WAVs. From the repository root run python -m http.server 8123 and then open http://localhost:8123/scripts/sounds/ab_click.html.

The bar

The orange dot is the downbeat at 1.0, the amber one is the middle at MEDIUM_GAIN, the grey ones are the plain beat at BEAT_GAIN. 4/4 is here on purpose and has no amber dot: it is one group, so there is nothing in the middle of it to mark. Pick a preset from the table below while it is looping and it swaps at the top of the next bar.
